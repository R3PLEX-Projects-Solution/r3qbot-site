The Problem

Quantity take-off should not lose its connection to the drawing.

Construction measurement remains highly dependent on repetitive tracing, checking and spreadsheet transfer. As drawings change, the reasoning behind a quantity can become increasingly difficult to follow.

01

Repetitive tracing

Professional time is consumed by repeated drawing measurement, labelling and data entry.

02

Revision rework

Drawing changes can trigger repeated checking and measurement across related quantities.

03

Weak traceability

Spreadsheet totals may not clearly show which drawing, geometry, rule or decision produced the quantity.

04

Fragmented review

Drawings, measurements, assumptions and approvals often sit across separate tools and records.

How It Works

AI assists the workflow. Professionals remain in control.

R3QBOT separates AI interpretation from authoritative measurement and professional approval.

01

Upload Drawing

Open supported construction PDF drawings in a structured project workspace.

02

Interpret

AI may assist with labels, drawing context and candidate measurable regions.

03

Extract Geometry

Geometry is created or validated as editable measurable regions.

04

Calculate

Approved geometry and explicit measurement rules drive deterministic calculations.

05

Review

Users inspect, correct, classify, accept or reject AI-assisted outputs.

06

Export & Audit

Approved quantities move into structured export while retaining their source and review history.

Why R3QBOT

Drawing intelligence designed around professional review.

R3QBOT is being developed as an assistive professional workspace rather than a black-box automatic measurement tool.

H

Human-Reviewed AI

AI suggestions remain draft assistance until reviewed, corrected where necessary and professionally approved.

↗

Traceability by Design

Measurements can remain linked to drawing, page, revision, geometry, classification and review status.

=

Deterministic Measurement

Quantities are calculated from approved geometry and explicit rules rather than free-form AI estimates.

M

Manual Fallback

Professional users retain the ability to create and correct measurements when AI assistance is unavailable or unsuitable.

Phase 1

Start narrow. Prove the complete workflow.

The immediate R3QBOT MVP focuses on architectural floor-area measurement from supported PDF drawings, with the goal of proving one reliable workflow from drawing upload to professionally reviewed export.

Current MVP Focus Building
  • ✓ Multi-page PDF drawing workspace
  • ✓ Drawing scale confirmation
  • ✓ Floor-area region creation and editing
  • ✓ AI-assisted candidate regions and labels where useful
  • ✓ Deterministic quantity calculation
  • ✓ Human review and approval
  • ✓ Structured Excel / CSV export and audit record
Future Expansion Later
  • → Beams
  • → Slabs and columns
  • → Walls, doors and windows
  • → Broader architectural and civil quantities
  • → Mechanical and electrical quantities
  • → Revision intelligence and connected project records
