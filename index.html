The Problem

Construction quantity take-off is still slow, fragmented and hard to audit.

Many professionals still work across PDFs, spreadsheets, manual tracing and disconnected notes. This creates repeated effort, weak traceability and higher risk during tender and review.

01

Manual and repetitive

Quantity surveyors and estimators spend significant time tracing, measuring, checking and re-entering the same information across tools.

02

Weak traceability

Spreadsheet totals often do not clearly show which drawing, page, room, assumption or revision supports each quantity.

03

Pressure to move faster

Short tender timelines increase the risk of missed regions, duplicated measurement and unclear professional decisions.

Core Workflow

R3QBOT keeps the drawing viewer at the centre of the workflow.

The product follows a disciplined process: AI assistance where useful, deterministic calculation where required, and human review before approval and export.

Step 01

Upload and review

Open multi-page construction drawings, confirm metadata and establish the working drawing context.

Step 02

Calibrate scale

Set or confirm drawing scale so the measurement basis is visible and reliable.

Step 03

Detect or draw geometry

AI may suggest measurable regions, but users can always draw, edit, split, merge or correct manually.

Step 04

Review and approve

Check quantities, record assumptions, deviations and warnings, then approve results with source traceability.

Step 05

Export with audit trail

Export approved quantities and registers to Excel or CSV while preserving revision and decision history.

Why R3QBOT

Not just AI output — a reviewable quantity knowledge base.

R3QBOT is designed as an AI-assisted professional tool, not an automatic black box. It helps teams move faster without removing Quantity Surveyor judgement.

◎

Drawing-linked traceability

Every measurable item can remain linked to the source drawing, page, revision, geometry and decision history.

◫

Human-reviewed AI

AI may interpret, detect or suggest, but it does not replace professional review or approve quantities on its own.

Σ

Deterministic calculation

Final quantity outputs should come from confirmed geometry, explicit logic and repeatable calculation rules.

↺

Audit and export readiness

Structured export, revision control and audit records support transparent checking and downstream commercial use.
