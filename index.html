The problem

Construction quantity take-off is still slow, fragmented and hard to audit.

Many professionals still work across PDFs, spreadsheets, manual tracing and disconnected notes. This creates repeated effort, weak traceability and higher risk during tender and review.

01

Manual and repetitive

Quantity surveyors and estimators spend significant time tracing, measuring, checking and re-entering the same information across tools.

02

Weak traceability

Spreadsheet totals often do not clearly show which drawing, page, room, assumption or revision supports each quantity.

03

Pressure to move faster

Short tender timelines increase the risk of missed regions, duplicated measurement and unclear professional decisions.

Today
  • PDFs, spreadsheets and notes in separate places
  • Totals without a visible source
  • Decisions recorded informally, if at all
With R3QBot
  • The drawing viewer at the centre of the workflow
  • Each quantity linked to page, revision and geometry
  • Assumptions, deviations and approvals on record

Deterministic calculation

Final quantity outputs should come from confirmed geometry, explicit logic and repeatable calculation rules.

// confirmed geometry
w = 5.000 m
d = 3.730 m
// rule: Floor Area
A = w × d
A = 18.65 m²
Same geometry + same rule = same quantity.Calculation runs on confirmed geometry and an explicit rule, so every figure can be checked and repeated.

Phase 1 MVP

Start narrow. Prove one real workflow properly.

The immediate goal is one complete and reliable workflow from PDF upload to professionally checked export. Floor area is the first commercial entry point.

What Phase 1 covers now

Current MVP
  • Project creation and multi-page PDF viewing
  • Scale calibration and page review
  • Manual and AI-assisted floor-area region workflow
  • Geometry editing, room labels and classification
  • Assumption, deviation and warning records
  • Review, approval and Excel/CSV export
