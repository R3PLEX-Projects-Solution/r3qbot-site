R3QBOT is an AI-assisted construction drawing intelligence and quantity take-off platform for Quantity Surveyors, estimators and project teams.

Currently building and validating the MVP

The Problem

Construction quantity take-off remains repetitive, time-consuming and difficult to audit. Measurements, classifications and spreadsheet totals can become disconnected from the drawings, revisions and professional decisions that produced them.

What R3QBOT Does

Drawing Intelligence

AI assists with interpreting construction drawings, measurable regions, labels and related information.

Traceable Quantities

Quantities remain connected to their source drawing, page, revision and measurable geometry.

Professional Review

AI-assisted outputs remain reviewable and editable before professional approval.

Structured Export

Approved quantities can be exported into structured Excel and CSV workflows with supporting audit information.

How It Works

AI interpretation → geometry extraction and validation → deterministic QS calculation → human review → professional approval → structured export and audit record

Built for Construction Professionals

Quantity Surveyors · Cost Consultants · Estimators · Contractors · Developers
